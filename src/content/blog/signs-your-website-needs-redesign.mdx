# 5 Signs Your Business Website Needs a Redesign

Your website is your 24/7 salesperson. But what if that salesperson is wearing a wrinkled shirt, giving outdated information, and speaking so slowly that customers give up and walk away?

Here are 5 signs that your website is costing you business — and what to do about it.

## Sign 1: It Loads Slowly on Mobile

If your website takes more than 3 seconds to load on a phone, **53% of visitors have already left**. Google also penalises slow websites in search rankings, making your SEO worse.

In 2025, over 70% of web traffic in India comes from mobile devices. A slow, non-mobile-optimised website is actively driving away the majority of your potential customers.

**What a modern website does**: Loads in under 2 seconds on any device, with optimised images, modern hosting, and performance-first code.

## Sign 2: It Looks Like It Was Built Before 2020

Web design trends move fast. A website that looked great in 2018 now looks outdated — and customers notice. More importantly, they associate your website's quality with your business's quality.

A modern, premium design tells customers: *this business takes quality seriously*.

## Sign 3: You Can't Update It Yourself

If changing a phone number or updating your menu requires calling your developer, you're operating with unnecessary friction. A modern website comes with a simple content management system where you make updates in minutes.

## Sign 4: It's Not Showing Up on Google

If searching for your business name or services doesn't bring up your website in the first few results, your SEO is broken. Common culprits include: missing meta tags, no Google Business Profile integration, slow load times, and poor mobile experience.

## Sign 5: Your Competitors' Sites Look Better

Open your top competitor's website. Does it make yours look dated? If customers are comparing you side-by-side, this perception gap directly affects who they choose.
